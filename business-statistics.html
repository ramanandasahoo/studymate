📖 Quick Revision

Statistics
Statistics is the science of collecting, organizing, presenting, analyzing and interpreting data.
Primary Data
Primary data is data collected directly by the investigator for a specific purpose.
Secondary Data
Secondary data is data already collected by someone else and used by the investigator.
Mean
Mean is the arithmetic average of a set of values.

Formula: Mean = ΣX / N
Median
Median is the middle value of data arranged in ascending or descending order.
Mode
Mode is the value that occurs most frequently in a data set.
Pie Chart
A pie chart is a circular diagram divided into sectors to represent data.
Tabulation
Tabulation is the systematic presentation of data in rows and columns.
